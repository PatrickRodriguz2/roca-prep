Radio drills: say it the RIC-21 way

Thirteen generated drills with fresh values every time: the 24-hour clock and the date-time group, numbers digit by digit, whole thousands, frequencies, money, altitudes, flight levels, headings, field elevations, UTC time, private aircraft call signs and the single station call. Each has a worked answer and its RIC-21 reference, and the wrong options are the slips candidates really make.

Mock exams scored against a 70% pass mark

Twenty-five questions with flagging and a question palette, and no feedback until you submit. The verdict is pass or fail on the whole paper at 70%, and the result shows where your marks went. The clock is a practice timer: no time limit is published for the real exam, so the app's 30 minutes is its own.

Review missed questions

After a mock, one tap takes every question you got wrong or left blank and keeps asking until each has been answered correctly once, with the explanation each time you slip.

The exam explained

A study card tells you what to expect from the real thing: a practical radio call, oral questions on procedure and regulations, and a written paper of 25 multiple-choice questions. Cards for every topic hold the must-know points, always open and offline.

Every answer explained

Practice questions are original, written from RIC-21 and checked against the Radiocommunication Act and Regulations and the current aviation publications. Each carries an explanation and a reference; where RIC-21 and today's practice differ, the answer follows RIC-21 and the explanation says what is different now. Missed questions return on a spaced schedule, and the dashboard estimates your readiness by area and by topic.

Free, private and offline

ROC-A Prep is free: no purchases, no subscription, no ads, no account, no analytics and no tracking. Everything works offline, your progress stays on your device, and the developer never sees it. For iPhone and iPad.

Your path to the PPL

ROC-A Prep is the second step. The same developer makes PSTAR Prep for the student pilot permit exam and PPL Prep for the private pilot written exam; the app lists the three in order, and each opens its own App Store page.
