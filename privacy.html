ROC-A Prep · iPhone & iPad

Privacy Policy

ROC-A Prep does not collect any personal information. There is no account, no analytics, no purchases, no advertising and no developer server. Your progress stays on your device.

What the app collects

Nothing. ROC-A Prep contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking technologies, and no third-party SDKs. It does not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal detail, because it has nowhere to send them.

What the app stores, and where

The app saves your study data so your progress survives between sessions. This includes:

  • which questions you have answered and whether you answered them correctly,
  • the spaced-review schedule built from those answers,
  • your mock exam results and timings,
  • your settings (light or dark appearance).

All of it is written to files inside the app's own storage on your device. It is never sent to any server belonging to the developer, and the developer cannot see it. The app does not sync progress between devices.

You can erase your progress at any time with "Reset all progress" in the Library tab. Deleting the app deletes it too.

Network access

The app itself contacts no server belonging to the developer. Every question, explanation and study note is bundled inside the app, and everything works entirely offline. The one exception is optional and is Apple's: if you tap another app in "Your path to the PPL", the system shows that app's App Store page, loaded by the App Store, not by ROC-A Prep. The app sends the App Store nothing about you.

Children

ROC-A Prep is a study aid for pilots and radio operators and is rated 4+. Since it collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date below will change.
