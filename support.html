ROC-A Prep · iPhone & iPad

Support

A study aid for the ROC-A, the Canadian aviation radio operator exam (study guide RIC-21).

Questions, bug reports, or a question you think is wrong? Email patrguzz@gmail.com. Corrections are especially welcome: please include the question and the reference you are working from (the RIC-21 section, the Radiocommunication Act or Regulations section, or the TC AIM section).

Requirements

iPhone or iPad running iOS 26.0 or later. Studying works entirely offline; the internet is used only if you tap another app in "Your path to the PPL", which opens that app's App Store page.

The app is free

There is nothing to buy, nothing locked, and no code to redeem: every question, drill, mock exam and study card is open from the first launch.

How the app works

Practise by topic with instant feedback: select an option, then Confirm answer to see the explanation and its reference. The Radio drills card in Practice runs a mix of thirteen generated drills with fresh values every time; each drill also appears in its own topic. Sit the 25-question mock exam; the pass mark is 70% on the whole paper, and the 30-minute clock is a practice timer, because no time limit is published for the real exam. On the result screen, Review missed questions asks every question you got wrong or left blank, and any you miss again, until each has been answered correctly once. Every answer is tracked: missed questions come back on a spaced schedule, and the Home tab estimates your readiness by area and by topic. The Library holds a card per topic and a card about the exam itself. The appearance setting (Automatic, Light or Dark) is in the Library.

RIC-21 and today's practice

RIC-21 (Issue 3, February 2010) is the study guide for the exam and is still the current issue. Where it and today's practice differ, the answer follows RIC-21 and the explanation notes the difference: the app teaches what the exam's study guide says and keeps you aware of what has changed.

Progress and resetting

Your progress is saved on your device only. To start over, use "Reset all progress" at the bottom of the Library tab. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

A question looks wrong?

Every question carries an explanation and a source reference. If you believe an answer conflicts with RIC-21, the Radiocommunication Act and Regulations or the current TC AIM, email the question text and your reference: verified corrections ship in the next update. Always treat the current official publications as authoritative.
